# User Stories

## Personas

1. **Primary Persona: Emma, the Budget-Conscious Professional**
   - Age: 28
   - Role: Early-career professional managing expenses and savings goals
   - Need: Track monthly spending, find smart saving opportunities, and receive simple guidance on budget health.

2. **Secondary Persona: Maya, the Busy Parent**
   - Age: 38
   - Role: Parent juggling family expenses and upcoming plans
   - Need: Understand spending patterns quickly, get easy-to-implement saving tips, and receive prompt conversational feedback.

3. **Tertiary Persona: Daniel, the Goal-Oriented Saver**
   - Age: 45
   - Role: Mid-career saver with long-term goals such as emergency fund and vacation
   - Need: Compare actual spending with targets, get AI insights on overspending categories, and stay motivated.

## Epics

- Epic 1: Budget tracking and expense categorization
- Epic 2: Goal creation and progress monitoring
- Epic 3: AI-driven saving tips and spending insights
- Epic 4: Conversational user responses and guidance

## User Stories

### Story 1: Add and categorize expenses

**As a** user,
**I want** to add expenses quickly and categorize them,
**so that** I can see where my money is going.

**Acceptance criteria**
- User can enter expense amount, category, date, and optional note.
- Expense is displayed in the monthly overview.
- User can assign or change categories after saving.

### Story 2: View budget summary

**As a** user,
**I want** to see a summary of my spending versus budget,
**so that** I can understand my current financial position.

**Acceptance criteria**
- App shows total spend, remaining budget, and major categories.
- Summary updates automatically after new expenses.
- App highlights overspending and underspending areas.

### Story 3: Set and track saving goals

**As a** user,
**I want** to define saving goals,
**so that** I can measure progress toward a target like an emergency fund.

**Acceptance criteria**
- User can create goals with a target amount and deadline.
- Progress is displayed visually.
- App notifies user when progress changes significantly.

### Story 4: Receive AI saving tips

**As a** user,
**I want** the app to suggest ways I can save money,
**so that** I can improve my spending habits.

**Acceptance criteria**
- AI provides at least one personalized tip per review.
- Tips are based on recent spending patterns and categories.
- Tips are actionable and easy to understand.

### Story 5: Get spending insights from AI

**As a** user,
**I want** the app to explain my spending trends,
**so that** I can make better financial decisions.

**Acceptance criteria**
- AI highlights top overspending categories.
- AI identifies irregular spending or patterns.
- Responses include supporting data points.

### Story 6: Ask questions and receive conversational responses

**As a** user,
**I want** to ask the app questions about my budget,
**so that** I can get immediate, friendly advice.

**Acceptance criteria**
- User can ask questions in a chat-like interface.
- AI responds with relevant budget information and recommendations.
- Responses reference the user’s own spending data when available.
